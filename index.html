FableScribe Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-15

FableScribe is built so that your writing and your world stay on your Mac. This policy explains, plainly, what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

FableScribe collects nothing. There are no analytics, no tracking, no accounts, and no developer-operated servers. Your manuscript, world bible, images, and settings live only on your device.

What data the app stores, and where

  • Your project data (books, chapters, characters, places, lore, factions, timelines, maps, relationships, drafts, AI conversations) is saved in a local database on your Mac.
  • Imported images (book covers, character portraits, maps) are copied into a local media folder on your Mac.
  • Preferences (appearance, editor settings, the address of your local AI server) are stored in standard macOS user defaults.

All of this stays on your device. FableScribe does not upload, sync, or transmit it to the developer or to any third party. You can back it up or delete it at any time using your own tools.

The AI assistant

FableScribe's AI assistant is optional and connects only to an Ollama server that you install and run on your own machine (by default http://localhost:11434).

  • When you send a message, the app builds context from your project (selected characters, places, lore, factions, and the current chapter) and sends it to your local Ollama server only.
  • This data never reaches the developer, and the developer operates no AI service. Requests stay on your computer (or wherever you have pointed your own Ollama instance).
  • If you change the server address in Settings to a remote Ollama you control, your data goes to that server under your control — not to the FableScribe developer. The app shows a warning whenever the address is not this Mac.
  • If Ollama is not installed, the AI features are simply unavailable; every other feature works unchanged.

Network use

The app makes no network connections of its own except to the Ollama server address you configure. It contacts no analytics, advertising, or telemetry endpoints.

Third parties

FableScribe includes no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. It shares no data with anyone.

Children

FableScribe collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Purchases

FableScribe is a one-time purchase through the Mac App Store. Payment is handled by Apple; the developer never sees your payment details. There are no subscriptions and no in-app purchases.
