FableScribe Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-11

FableScribe stores your writing and world on your Mac and lets you choose where AI requests, exports, and backups go. This policy explains, plainly, what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

FableScribe collects nothing. There are no analytics, no tracking, no accounts, and no developer-operated servers. Your manuscript, world bible, images, and settings are stored on your device. Optional remote Ollama requests and user-selected exports/backups have the recipients described below.

What data the app stores, and where

  • Your project data (books, chapters, characters, places, lore, factions, timelines, maps, relationships, drafts, AI conversations) is saved in a local database on your Mac.
  • Imported images (book covers, character portraits, maps) are copied into a local media folder on your Mac.
  • Preferences (appearance, editor settings, the address of your local AI server) are stored in standard macOS user defaults.

FableScribe does not send project content to the developer.

Deletion, copies, and retention

  • Deleting a project in FableScribe removes its database records and app-managed media after the deletion is saved. FableScribe also asks macOS to remove that project's Spotlight entries. macOS may retain system caches or backup snapshots according to your Mac and backup settings.
  • Backups are independent copies at a location you choose. Deleting a project or the app does not delete those copies; delete them at their destination when you no longer want them. A destination provider such as a cloud-synced folder may retain or synchronize them under its own policy.
  • Exports are independent document copies at a location you choose. FableScribe does not track or delete them after export; you and any destination provider control their retention.
  • Spotlight receives project titles and short content summaries for macOS system search. FableScribe requests removal when indexed content or a project is deleted and rebuilds the index from the current library. macOS controls Spotlight storage and deletion timing.
  • Removing FableScribe is controlled by macOS and does not automatically remove files you exported, backups you created, copies synchronized by other providers, or data retained by a remote Ollama operator.

The AI assistant

FableScribe's AI assistant is optional and connects to the Ollama server address you configure (by default http://localhost:11434 on this Mac).

  • When you send a message, the app builds context from your project (selected characters, places, lore, factions, and the current chapter) and sends it to your configured Ollama server.
  • This data never reaches the developer, and the developer operates no AI service. Requests stay on your computer (or wherever you have pointed your own Ollama instance).
  • If you choose a remote Ollama address, manuscript context and chapters go to that server. Its operator controls retention and access. HTTPS encrypts transport; HTTP does not. Settings warns when you select a remote address.
  • Deleting a project, conversation, or FableScribe itself cannot delete request data retained by a remote Ollama server. Use that server operator's deletion and retention controls.
  • If Ollama is not installed, the AI features are simply unavailable; every other feature works unchanged.

Network use

The app makes no network connections of its own except to the Ollama server address you configure. It contacts no analytics, advertising, or telemetry endpoints.

Third parties

FableScribe includes no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. A remote Ollama operator or an export/backup destination provider may receive content when you choose those destinations.

Children

FableScribe collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Purchases

FableScribe is a one-time purchase through the Mac App Store. Payment is handled by Apple; the developer never sees your payment details. There are no subscriptions and no in-app purchases.
