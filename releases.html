What changed in each version of FableScribe, newest first. The same notes appear in the app under Help ▸ What’s New.

FableScribe 3.9 — One screen grammar, Revise mode and a smarter editor

Released 6 September 2026

  • One screen grammar in every mode — Rail, Sidebar, canvas and Inspector work the same way in Write, Map, Timeline, Relations, Corkboard, Threads, Scenes and Board; sheets, cards, empty states and findings rows share one shape.
  • Keyboard shortcuts, declared once — Every binding lives in one catalog; ⌘F is Find, Focus mode is ⌃⌘F, and the cheat sheet and tooltips read the same entry.
  • Back and Forward — Step through the workspace history on ⌘[ and ⌘], across chapters, cards and modes.
  • Revise mode — A dimmed page, the chapter's findings in a list, and a side-by-side compare with any earlier draft.
  • Annotations — Margin notes anchored to the text, listed in the Inspector and Binder, exported as a revision report and round-tripped with Scrivener.
  • Editor themes, fonts and typewriter highlight — Paper, Sepia and Mono themes, a font family picker, and a highlight that dims everything but the current sentence or paragraph.
  • Status strip and markup bar — A goal ring and live counts under the page, and a floating bar for bold, italic and links when text is selected.
  • Deadline and daily requirement — Set a book deadline and the daily goal computes itself from what is left.
  • Keywords and Binder filters — Tag chapters and scenes with keywords, filter the Binder by them and save the filters you use.
  • Export presets — Presets with a live preview, and Copy Chapter as rich text or Markdown.
  • Share, Shortcuts and Spotlight — Send pages and images to Research from any app, run New Chapter or Start a Sprint from Shortcuts, and find chapters and world entries in Spotlight.
  • Start simple — A new book opens in Write alone; planning modes appear as the world fills in.
  • What's New — This sheet, once per update, and a public releases page.

FableScribe 2.5 — Relations, boards and imports

Released 3 September 2026

  • Relations web, redesigned — Search, legend chips and a focus mode in the sidebar; cluster-aware overview and ego layouts; export as PNG or vector PDF.
  • One sidebar for Relations and Map — The family tree, the map legend, filters and routes, and the lineage root picker all live in the sidebar now.
  • Board — A freeform canvas of scene cards joined by yarn, with marquee selection, as its own rail mode (⌘8).
  • Faction territories — Draw, edit and list territory polygons on the Map with vertex handles.
  • Scene ledger — Each scene carries a date, a place and a cast, and the continuity checks read them.
  • Scenes board — POV colours and a word-count curve across the whole manuscript.
  • Prose metrics — Echoes, adverbs, sentence rhythm and dialogue balance, right in the editor and without AI.
  • Find & Replace — Project-wide search gains a Replace mode with preview and scopes, applied as one undoable change.
  • Scrivener import — Bring a Scrivener 3 project in as a book, chapters and scenes included.
  • German everywhere — Every new surface ships with German strings.

FableScribe 2.3 — Safer deletes and lighter media

Released 1 September 2026

  • Transactional deletion — Deleting a book, a series or a cover now commits or rolls back as one unit, with a single retryable alert when it fails.
  • Media storage — Images are compressed and downsampled on import, decoded off the main thread, and orphaned files are swept at launch and from Settings.
  • Faster typing — The book word total is cached and the typing path stays on the main actor.
  • Sample world — Characters carry aliases, and the sample world's gaps are filled in.
